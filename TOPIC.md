Network Intrusion & Cyber Attack Detection
Task: Preprocess network traffic data (encode protocols, normalize packet stats); combine clustering (DBSCAN/K-Means) and distance metrics (Mahalanobis/Euclidean) to isolate anomalous connections; compare unsupervised anomaly detection against supervised models; evaluate detection capability on novel attack signatures.
Dataset: [Kaggle - NSL-KDD Dataset](https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/hassan06/nslkdd)
